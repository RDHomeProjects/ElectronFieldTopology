## D. Behavioural curves and adaptive sampling

Use actual spacing as the varied parameter and matched-horizon median force, median maximum acceleration and their normalized joint score as responses. Fit linear, quadratic and exponential models only with at least five distinct matched points. Require leave-one-out range-normalized RMSE≤0.25 and skill≥0.10 over a constant-response model. Models with invalid/negative predictions or over25% disagreement cannot propose a fitted point. The fit-error threshold is unchanged from the previous campaign and is not increased to produce a result.

At most two additional odd meshes, within129–193, may be measured. Prefer a component-feasible, jointly supported interpolation when validated models agree; otherwise concentrate on measured changes of slope and gaps. Such exploratory points are explicitly labelled and do not claim an inflection or optimum. Unsupported extrapolation beyond the resource/grid bound is recorded as requiring another experiment. Failed fits remain failed fits.

Select a longer-confirmation candidate only if it improves the joint score by at least5% relative to the reset129 baseline at BOTH30 and80 accepted updates, while neither component worsens by more than10%. If one qualifies, compare that measured mesh and the baseline at120 updates by extending their existing trajectories. No selected point is called a universal optimum. A ranking reversal, failed fit, admission loss, stall or unmatched horizon is retained.

## E. Budget, gates and interpretation

The maximum is720 newly charged optimizer attempts: 5×80 registered meshes +80 exact-memory control +2×80 adaptive meshes +2×40 longer confirmation. Work is charged before execution, including failed/interrupted work; resume never refunds attempts. There is no overall wall-time cap and unused budget need not be consumed. Preflight is read-only and takes zero optimization steps. Physical-time steps are zero throughout.

Original readiness gates remain: relative force≤1e-4; maximum acceleration≤1e-3; approximate absolute charge error≤5%; positive cone; unit error<1e-10; tail≤0.00075; guard≤0.001. The30° neighbor-angle reference from the reviewed numerical literature is a resolution warning, not a new admission gate. All quantities are model quantities or fractions, not SI electron units. No fitted value grants admission.

Falling energy, interpolation improvement or an80/120-step residual advantage does not establish convergence of stationary solutions. Report actual final and retained best states separately, fixed-horizon differences separately from initialization effects, and any energy/shape discrepancies as provisional while residual gates fail. Perturbation-return, full lowest-mode analysis, new minimizers, domain/seed/model changes and motion remain deferred pending these findings.
