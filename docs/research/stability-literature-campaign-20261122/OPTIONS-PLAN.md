# Controlled option coverage

All arms start independently from authenticated Q21 iteration 335, except the two explicitly declared fresh rational-map configurations. Comparisons of a transformed field use restart-CG as their matched same-method baseline. Exact-CG retains full conjugacy memory. All other arms reset memory and keep the source alpha, grid and box unless the arm names that variable. Optimizer counters start at335 to align the inherited CG restart schedule; a fresh state is never claimed to have undergone335 prior updates.

Maximum new optimizer allocation:2300 attempts across19 declared arms. Every attempt is charged before execution and remains charged if interrupted. No unused budget is silently moved between arms. Every accepted update is measured. The first20 attempts establish a local history; subsequent5–10-attempt blocks use measured joint progress and validated linear/quadratic/exponential fits. At least80 attempts are allowed before a repeated plateau decision can stop the slow-relaxation diagnostic; three repeated joint-progress failures stop it. Topology/representation failure, line-search stall, resource limits and measured stationarity stop sooner. All stationarity and boundary failures remain failures.

The initial-alpha growth, tangent projection, preconditioner and line-search constants are held equal across the matched optimizer comparisons. L-BFGS keeps at most three transported curvature pairs. The legacy exact-CG implementation is bundled byte-for-byte; alternative methods are new and validated with small-grid controls, not asserted to reproduce a particular published implementation.
