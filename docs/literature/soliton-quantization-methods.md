The current metric is derived from our existing action, with fixed K and c=1,
and checked against direct kinetic energy. We measure finite-box inertia sensitivity
before treating it as input to any semiclassical spectrum. Full configuration-space
topology must constrain a reduced family; naive rigid-rotor quantization can give
spurious states. A classical orbit animation does not measure an FR sign.

Electromagnetic charge, magnetic moment, quantum probabilities and detector outcomes
are not supplied by these three methods. The wavefunction in a collective-coordinate
calculation is on configuration space; it is not the scalar energy density of one
field snapshot. Any future quantization/probe law must be explicit, with inputs and
predictions distinguished. Q1 is a useful low-complexity method benchmark; this does
not identify it as an electron or discard Q18's preserved negative findings.
