# ADR-0005 — Resume bounded S4-B with recorded literature limits

Date: 31 October 2026 (Australia/Melbourne). Scope authorised by the maintainer's instruction: continue S4-B and integrate newly identified papers, updating the main bibliography. Implementation and scientific interpretation remain subject to PR review.

The prose transfer and 1% dataset publication did not complete the literature review. The maintainer's new instruction explicitly resumes S4-B despite that outstanding work. This supersedes the earlier operational S4-B hold only for the bounded work below; it does not retrospectively certify either literature pass, authorise level-4 acquisition or select extended dynamics.

Decision: use the existing four regular Bateman families, fixed off-core seeds/times, explicit zero/near-zero and observation budgets, complete section-return ledgers, independent polygon invariants and source-mapped initial-condition sampling. Preserve every unresolved result. Promote influential recovered papers into the main register/BibTeX with their actual review depths, and add the equation source for the independent Fox-colouring instrument. Do not substitute an abstract for an equation audit.

Alternatives: keep the operational hold despite the new instruction; treat the finite literature sample as a completed comprehensive review; or start broad optimisation. The first would ignore the maintainer's instruction; the latter two are scientifically unsupported. Bounded implementation permits reviewable progress while retaining uncertainty.

Consequences: full literature applicability/version reconciliation and impact closure remain unfinished; level 4 stays deferred. Determinant/projection agreement is not a full knot-name or continuum-isotopy certificate. S5–S7 model, energy, charge and quantum gates are unchanged. No inference of binding or electron properties follows.
