# ADR-0007 — Tightened milestones after workstation continuation

Date: 1 November 2026 (Australia/Melbourne). Status: accepted scope following the maintainer's instruction to record and merge the current results before continuing down the tightened milestone path. This narrows research priorities; it accepts no new physical theory.

## Context and decision

The [workstation record](../../reports/s5-a/workstation-2026-11-01/README.md) supports a stationary coarse Q22 cable and a stationary finer Q18 linked alternative. Finer Q22 residuals and both domain checks remain open. The source hyperbolic local minimum was not reproduced. More charge labels alone would not answer the project's charge, confinement or relativistic-dynamics questions.

1. Preserve and merge the measured partial outcome, complete provenance and negative findings. Code acceptance and scientific reproduction acceptance are separate.
2. Bound the remainder of S5-A: polish existing Q22 161³/193³ fields; check a larger domain at matched spacing; assess a stationary same-charge comparator when feasible. Retain the original 1e-3 residual, 2% mesh/domain energy, 5% scale/virial and 3% source-energy limits, with explicit topology/charge checks. No coefficient fitting. A budget-limited result closes as partial, without an indefinite search.
3. Keep the Q18 linked alternative and failed hyperbolic-basin attempt as findings. A different relaxation/seed study requires a specific bounded scientific question; do not force preimage topology.
4. Next, audit a charge-capable relativistic proposal at equation level: changed assumptions, fields/action and conventions; electric-charge/flux map; finite energy and scale setting; characteristic/causal and stability conditions; supplied parameters and independent predictions; and what interaction experiment could falsify it. Faber–Kobushkin is an analytical candidate, not a selected numerical model.
5. Admit another Q only when it resolves a stated mechanism, convergence or stability decision. A wider knot catalogue, new dynamics simulator and level-4 expansion are deferred.

Use a new task branch and a checkpoint-preserving follow-up plan after the evidence merge. Preserve the original campaign and absolute checkpoint paths; do not overwrite fields or reset historical budgets. Large numerical work remains explicitly bounded and local. The final S5-A ledger must state each passed, failed and unresolved gate even if no complete reproduction is obtained.
