## ADR-0001 — Initial research baseline

Date: 2026-10-31. Status: adopted for the initial investigation.

Begin with known source-free Maxwell knots, analytical diagnostics and independent verification. Defer broad searches and modified theories until their equations and constraints are explicit. This gives a reference baseline and prevents imposed charge or confinement from being mistaken for emergence.

Use Python-first reusable modules, with notebooks as explanations. GitHub stores source and reports; Actions runs bounded validation. Heavy experiments remain manual.

Software: Apache-2.0. Original prose/figures: CC-BY-4.0. Third-party works retain their licences.

Consequence: the baseline cannot establish a nonzero Maxwell electric charge from smooth source-free data. This is an expected control, not a reason to suppress the research question.
