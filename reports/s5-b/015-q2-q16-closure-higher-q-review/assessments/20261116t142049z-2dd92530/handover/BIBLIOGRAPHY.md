# Primary references and applicability

Accessed during this review on 17 November 2026 (Australia/Melbourne). This is a focused feasibility review, not an exhaustive literature search.

1. Paul Sutcliffe (2007), *Knots in the Skyrme–Faddeev model*, Proceedings of the Royal Society A 463, 3001–3020. [arXiv:0705.1468](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.1468), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2007.0038). Numerical minima through Q16 and rational-map initial conditions; local versus putative global minima distinguished. Does not certify our mesh, optimizer or electron interpretation.
2. Paul Jennings (2015), *Cabling in the Skyrme–Faddeev model*, Journal of Physics A 48, 315401. [arXiv:1412.8597v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.8597v2), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/48/31/315401), [author institution record](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/output/1424933/cabling-in-the-skyrme-faddeev-model). Cable and hyperbolic static configurations. Paper abstract and publication metadata checked; full-paper access was unavailable through the retrieval route used here.
3. Paul Jennings (2015), Durham doctoral thesis, chapter4, especially §4.3, printed pp92–93. [Author thesis](https://etheses.durham.ac.uk/id/eprint/11161/1/thesis_jennings.pdf). The charge-specific summary below comes from indexed primary thesis text; the PDF itself could not be opened through this retrieval route. These are the author's reported numerical results, not new reproductions. Before implementing a particular rational map, read the complete equations, tables and normalization directly.
4. J. Hietarinta, J. Palmu, J. Jäykkä and P. Pakkanen (2012), *Scattering of knotted vortices (Hopfions) in the Faddeev–Skyrme model*, New Journal of Physics 14, 013013. [arXiv:1108.5551](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5551), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/14/1/013013). Numerical dynamics and scattering exist in this model; neither this paper nor static cable results validate long-lived motion for our high-Q fields.
5. H. Kedia, I. Bialynicki-Birula, D. Peralta-Salas and W. T. M. Irvine (2013), *Tying knots in light fields*, Physical Review Letters 111, 150404. [arXiv:1302.0342](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.0342), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.150404). Exact source-free null Maxwell fields with torus-knot/link field lines and topology-preserving evolution. Different field equations and classification from the neutral Skyrme–Faddeev survey.
