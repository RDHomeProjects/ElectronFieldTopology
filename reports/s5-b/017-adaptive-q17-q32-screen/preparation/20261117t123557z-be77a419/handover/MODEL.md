# Adaptive higher-Q screening contract

The fixed neutral Skyrme–Faddeev action, D4 positive sigma term, float64 projected RK4 and established projected Sobolev-CG optimizer are retained. Exact numerical source and lock hashes are checked. The complete local numerical source tree, actual checkout commit, package, plan, backend and thread identities bind the run and its checkpoints.

The prior published reconciliation supports the historical Q22 bounded static benchmark and selected Q17/Q19 cell-functional branches. It does not admit them under D4 or establish physical-time stability. Q18's hyperbolic reproduction remains incomplete. Q8's tested internal rotation offered no useful residual rescue. The Q2–Q16 allocation closure, original failures and deferred work remain unchanged.

The native bridge replays cell energy and the historical CPU tangent-gradient RMS, normalized over free components by K h-cubed, against the authenticated endpoint record. D4 relative force and maximum acceleration are separately labelled and measured on the identical field. The metrics are not interchangeable thresholds. Source-field absence is a documented deferral; source-hash/operator inconsistency is an instrument fault.

Static admission remains relative force <=1e-4, maximum acceleration <=1e-3, Hopf magnitude within 5%, positive corner cone and unit error <1e-10. The Hopf integral is finite-grid Coulomb-gauge quadrature, not an exact integer certificate. Boundary admission uses width-two fraction <=0.00075 and width-three fraction <=0.001. Optimization, including its existing 200-iteration conjugacy reset, is not model time; adaptive batch boundaries do not themselves reset memory or alpha.

Adaptive fits use recent within-epoch measurements of force, acceleration and energy. Candidate models are linear, quadratic and exponential with a fitted nonnegative floor. Two causal validation origins withhold later points; poor forecasts and all rejections are reported. Model spread limits the usable extrapolation horizon but is not a statistical confidence interval. Minimum exploratory allocations cover insufficient data. Recent joint force/acceleration progress and nonincreasing energy constrain extensions. Repeated measured plateaus, representation loss, optimizer failure and explicit budgets end a route as unresolved, not physically impossible. Sampling densifies around observed slope/curvature turns, threshold approaches and disagreement. No predicted crossing, small energy decrease, raw energy ranking across Q or fitted oscillation proves admission or stability.

Fresh initialization families are declared in CATALOGUE.json. The common fixed profile is an engineering choice, not an assertion that it is optimal for each Q. Native retained fields, fresh starts, prolonged mesh starts and centered domain embeddings carry separate provenance. Core preimages may reconnect; the campaign does not lock a knot family. Final curve identification is deferred until a candidate warrants the more expensive topology work.

Mesh and domain comparisons change one physical grid parameter at a time. Both compared endpoints must be stationary and represented before a <=2% energy difference is labelled a passing scalar comparison. Two such samples are not a continuum extrapolation or a complete isolation study. Capacity limits preserve every omitted survivor as a deferral. The first screen may miss a suitable branch because the initialization family, resolution or finite budget is inadequate; it never rules out a Hopf sector.

The optional motion stage uses projected cubic boosted data with Lorentz contraction and analytic field rate. Its shape gate uses the historical normalized trilinear coasting reference, independently compared with the installed reference initializer at t0. It makes no fitted translation, phase or rotation correction. Lab-grid Hopf quadrature uses the actual axis spacings; its isotropic limit matches the retained diagnostic. Original motion limits remain energy drift 0.1%, momentum/E0 drift 0.1%, shape 1%, speed 1%, tail 0.1%, positive represented Hopf and unit/tangency <1e-10. Tail safety reserve is 0.09%; admission reserve is 0.075%. Tail is sampled every physical 0.01. Milestones T=.05/.1/.2 retain the original gates; a failed speed flag becomes stop-eligible at T=.1. Passing primary trajectories receive identical-grid half-dt checks with field/rate differences <=1e-4/1e-3. Two dt values do not define a convergence order. Long-time motion, physical stability, electric charge and electron properties remain separate gates.

Backend choice is based on actual synchronized measurements with CPU audit and I/O costs. CUDA energy/gradient and short optimization parity are required at each geometry; actual nonzero-rate acceleration/RK4 parity is required before CUDA motion. CPU metrics govern promotions. Qualified device and backend choices are retained on resume. Any numerical parity mismatch is a fault, not permission to fall back silently.
