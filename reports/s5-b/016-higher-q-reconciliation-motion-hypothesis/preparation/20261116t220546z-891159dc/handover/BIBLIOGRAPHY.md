# Primary bibliography and applicability

Checked17 November2026 Australia/Melbourne. This focused review supplements the existing project bibliography; it is not an exhaustive literature search.

1. P. Jennings, *Cabling in the Skyrme–Faddeev model*, J. Phys. A48 (2015)315401. [Version2 full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.8597), [abstract and DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.8597). Equation2.1, numerical method in §4, Table1 and hyperbolic-knot discussion checked directly for this review. This improves on the previous closure review's abstract/thesis access limitation; the older assessment is retained unchanged.
2. D. Harland, J. Jäykkä, Y. Shnir and M. Speight, *Isospinning hopfions*, J. Phys. A46 (2013)225402. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.2923), [abstract and DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2923). Equations1.1 and2.5–2.15 and the frequency-bound discussion checked. The model includes a potential; its existence/stability results are not directly transferable to our massless neutral action.
3. R. A. Battye and M. Haberichter, *Classically isospinning Hopf solitons*, Phys. Rev. D87 (2013)105003. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.6803). Abstract checked: massive-model isospin changes configuration types and relative energies. No local reproduction or full-paper numerical replay is claimed here.
4. J. Hietarinta, J. Palmu, J. Jäykkä and P. Pakkanen, *Scattering of knotted vortices (Hopfions) in the Faddeev–Skyrme model*, New J. Phys.14 (2012)013013. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1108.5551), [abstract and DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5551). §3.2 describes relaxed minima followed by Lorentz boosts and scattering. Supports separating static preparation from actual physical-time dynamics.
5. H. Kedia, I. Bialynicki-Birula, D. Peralta-Salas and W. T. M. Irvine, *Tying knots in light fields*, Phys. Rev. Lett.111 (2013)150404. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1302.0342), [abstract and DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.0342). Source-free null electromagnetic knot families with topology-preserving evolution. These are different fields/equations from the neutral unit-vector model; persistent topology alone does not establish an electron-like localized object.
