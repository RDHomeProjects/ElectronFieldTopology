# Separate Maxwell Yee benchmark

Source map: Valverde et al., arXiv:2003.01896v1, Section II B, Eq. (11), and Eqs. (1)-(5) for the analytical (1,1) Hopfion. Main bibliography valverde2020 already exists. Our index implementation is a local derivation of the Yee curl updates, not a byte reproduction of that paper's code. Existing Fourier solver source is evolution.py; analytical fields are fields.py.

Natural units c=epsilon0=mu0=1. E components live on their oriented cell edges at integer time; B components on faces at half time. Initialize E at t=0 and B at t=-dt/2 using exact fields at each component's spatial offsets. Update B with forward curl(E), then E with backward curl(B). Compare final E at T and B at T-dt/2 to their respective exact values; do not interpolate them together to assert energy/nullness. The step uses dt/h <= 0.25/sqrt(3), within the vacuum Yee stability bound. Requested T=0.2; dt chosen to reach T exactly.

Plan: 33/49/65 cells per side, side 8, offsets 0/quarter/half/three-quarter cell in x/z, same analytical profile. Periodic transverse plane waves independently check propagation and refinement; collocated PSATD plane-wave errors are checked against the same exact wave at common time. Hopfion boundaries overwrite the two outer layers with exact time-dependent values so wraparound differences are confined to those layers. Error is measured inside the fixed physical cube |coordinate_i|<=2. This is an exact-boundary manufactured-solution comparison, not an open/absorbing isolated simulation, topology certification or matched-boundary PSATD Hopfion comparison.

Report phase errors and resolution trends for each component/time and combined native space-time norm. No conservation, photon/electron, charge, spin or field-line-knot conclusions are inferred. A future whole-box flux/helicity test needs synchronized observables and an independently validated boundary treatment. FDTD and SF are different equations; Maxwell success cannot close Q3 SF gates.
