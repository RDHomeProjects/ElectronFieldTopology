What the measurements show

At fixed dt=1/240, Hopfion synchronized native field error grows from 0.394673% at T=0.2 to 0.975997% at T=0.5 and 1.945625% at T=1. The packet grows from 0.0199624% to 0.0496548% and 0.0985835%. The observed duration dependence is close to linear with a small, resolved curvature; an exponential is a poorer empirical description over this tested range. No extrapolation to long-time physical instability is justified.

At T=1, reducing dt from 1/240 to 1/960 changes the Hopfion error from 1.945625% to 1.947248%, and packet error from 0.0985835% to 0.0986776%. Total errors change by only about 0.08–0.10% relative and slightly increase. Time refinement alone is therefore unlikely to remove the dominant error at this grid. A spatial/discrete-initialization/observation contribution is a leading hypothesis, not a demonstrated decomposition. Do not select the largest dt just because cancellation gives it a slightly lower total norm.

The Fourier packet reference agrees with its exact finite-mode field at roughly 6e-16. At T=1 its common-grid difference from Yee is about 0.19218%, while Yee's synchronized native error is about 0.09858%. These are different observables: the common-grid result includes the linear interpolation used to combine staggered components. Keep those responses separate when comparing future curves.

Initial normalized discrete divergence is about 0.03350 for Hopfion and 0.002065 for the packet. Its subsequent change is at most about 1.36e-13. The scheme preserves an initially nonzero discrete residual; it does not establish zero discrete divergence. This is not an emergent charge observation. The periodic modified-energy invariant drifts by at most 2.22e-16, while synchronized collocated physical-energy change is about 1.73e-7 at most. A discrete invariant and physical energy are distinct diagnostics.

The flux quadrature gaps decrease by approximately a factor of four for each halving of dt, but the balance defects approach nonzero values. At T=1 and dt=1/960, Hopfion defect magnitude is about 0.03028% of initial cube energy, or 1.137% of the transported-energy normalization. Packet values are 0.05752% and 1.865%. That combination points toward remaining spatial/observation balance error, rather than a flux-time quadrature problem alone. It is not an isolated outer-boundary conservation certificate.

The chosen Hopfion y-axis sweep shows no resolved dependence in its aggregate field-error response: the observed spread is about 1e-17 in fractional error. Packet cubic rotations agree to rounding scale. These narrow results do not establish arbitrary-angle isotropy for other fields, local directional diagnostics or boundaries. No further angle budget is warranted for this same aggregate response.

Sampler correction before another handover

The manufactured x³ inflection control passes, but the saved qualification also labels two roots around ±1.05e-8 as turning points. Those are numerical splits of a stationary-inflection root; slope reversal was not required by the root-based classifier. Before further feature-driven runs, require a derivative sign change across a candidate extremum, merge near-coincident roots, and keep inflection/turning-point labels separate. No recorded field errors or fit-validation outcomes are invalidated by this label issue.
